# **App Name**: Wubanchi

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog: Browse and view detailed descriptions, images, and pricing for various poultry products (eggs, meat, feed, etc.).
- Product Filtering & Search: Allow users to easily find specific poultry products by category, type, or search terms.
- AI-Powered Poultry Tips: An AI tool that provides tailored advice on poultry care, feed management, or product preparation based on user-submitted queries.
- About Us & Farm Story: Informative section detailing the farm's history, practices, values, and contact information.
- Contact & Inquiry Form: A simple form for users to send messages, ask questions, or inquire about custom orders.
- Responsive Design: Ensure optimal viewing and interaction experience across all devices, including mobile phones and desktop computers.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A warm, muted goldenrod yellow (#3BAEDA) evoking fresh eggs and sunshine on a farm, offering a cheerful yet earthy tone.
- Background color: A very light cream with a subtle hint of yellow (#fdfae7), providing a clean and inviting canvas for content in a light theme.
- Accent color: A soft, peachy-orange (#1F1A17) that complements the primary yellow, used for calls-to-action and highlights, suggesting natural ripeness.
- Headline and body font: 'PT Sans' (humanist sans-serif) for its readability, modern look, and a touch of warmth that fits the natural theme.
- Use simple, clean line icons related to farm life and poultry, such as chickens, eggs, feed bags, and contact symbols, to enhance navigation and visual appeal.
- Implement a responsive grid layout with ample whitespace, ensuring product images are prominent and content is easily digestible on both mobile and desktop screens.
- Incorporate subtle hover animations on interactive elements like product cards and buttons, providing gentle visual feedback without distracting from the content.
